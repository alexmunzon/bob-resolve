## PR 10: Held-out two-agency world from agency-data-commons (2026-10-05)

- bob-resolve now depends on agency-data-commons pinned to tag v0.2.0. The pin is a local path for now and becomes the GitHub URL at the same tag once that repo exists.
- New `bob-resolve run --world multi-a-b`: agency A (the snapshot) plus agency B, generated by the commons generator and checked byte for byte against the files commons commits.
- The scorecard of that world is labeled "held-out: generated by agency-data-commons v0.2.0, never used to tune the matcher" and adds look-alike merges by type and recall per injector type.
- The matcher was not changed. It meets the precision, blocking, and overall recall targets, but merges some twins and look-alikes and leaves recall on the 320 cross-agency pairs below 0.90. Those two tests are marked as known misses for a later fix.
- The snapshot and derived numbers are now pinned by a regression test.
